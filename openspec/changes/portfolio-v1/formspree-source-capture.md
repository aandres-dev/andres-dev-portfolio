## Account limits

- **Source URL:** https://help.formspree.io/articles/account-management/account-limits
- **Publisher:** Formspree Help Center
- **Title:** Account limits

> Account limits define the resources available to you, though every Formspree plan includes **unlimited forms and projects**. On the Free plan, you can link up to two email addresses for notifications and store 30 days of submission history. Paid plans offer expanded capabilities, including unlimited linked emails, longer data retention, file upload storage, and team collaboration features.

> Submission allowances vary by plan, starting at 50 per month on the Free tier.

## Protecting your Forms with Cloudflare Turnstile

- **Source URL:** https://help.formspree.io/articles/form-and-project-settings/protecting-your-forms-with-cloudflare-turnstile
- **Publisher:** Formspree Help Center
- **Title:** Protecting your Forms with Cloudflare Turnstile

> Formspree supports Turnstile out of the box — just configure your keys and enable CAPTCHA in your form settings.

> Under the **CAPTCHA** section, make sure CAPTCHA protection is **enabled**.
>
> Click **Adjust settings**, select **Cloudflare Turnstile**, and paste your **Secret Key** in the provided field.
>
> Save your changes.

> When a user submits the form, the Turnstile widget automatically generates a token (`cf-turnstile-response`) that is verified by Formspree using your Secret Key.
